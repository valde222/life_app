# Swipe row

Swiping a row does the most common thing without opening it. Both directions always do something.

- Todo: right puts it on Today; left moves it (Inbox) or opens its actions sheet (lists).
- Quick note in the inbox: right keeps it, left archives it.
- The action sits under the row in `accent` (Archive in `muted`), with an icon and one word.
- Every swipe can be undone from the toast.
