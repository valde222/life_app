# List row

One list inside a group box, on Lists and in the desktop sidebar.

- A 12 × 28 colour bar from the list's pair: `*-bg` fill with a thin `*-fg` edge. The name carries the meaning; the bar only helps find it.
- Name in `row` style, counts in `meta` on the right ("5 todos, 9 notes", or "Empty").
- Rows share one card with `divider` hairlines.
