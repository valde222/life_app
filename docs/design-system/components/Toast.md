# Toast

A short confirmation after an action, with one way back.

- Sits 164px from the bottom, above the nav. `accent` background, `radius-feature`.
- Two or three words: "Kept as a note", "On Today", "Pay taxes done". No second line.
- Undo is the white button on the right.
- After ticking a todo, an "Add note" button sits before Undo and opens a new note already linked to that todo.
- Disappears by itself after a few seconds.
