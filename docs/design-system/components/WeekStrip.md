# Week strip

The top of Agenda: seven days, today marked, a dot on days with something planned.

- One white card, `radius-row`, seven equal cells.
- The selected day is filled with `accent`. Days with items get a 4px dot.
- Follows the list below it: scrolling the list moves the strip. Swiping the strip changes week.
- A handle under the strip pulls it open into the month. Tapping the month name opens month and year.
