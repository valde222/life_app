# Sheet

Everything that asks one question slides up from the bottom: capture, pickers, row actions, confirmations.

- `bg` background, `radius-sheet` top corners, a 36 × 4 `control` grab handle, 32px bottom padding.
- The screen behind dims with `scrim`.
- Title uses `sheet-title` and ends with a period when it is a statement ("Time.", "Changed on two devices.").
- Options sit in one white card with `divider` hairlines. Destructive options go in their own card below, in `overdue-icon`.
- At most one primary button. Cancel is a secondary button at the bottom.
